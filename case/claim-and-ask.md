# Claim and ask

## Decision, audience, action
- **DECISION:** a moneyline pick: which of the two teams will win, made before the game starts.
- **AUDIENCE:** a sports bettor (role).
- **ACTION:** bet the moneyline on the home team with more rest than its opponent.

## My claim
> A sports bettor who bets the moneyline before kickoff on the home team with more rest backs the side that wins 56.3 percent of the time, against 53.7 percent on equal rest.

**Test**
- Specific finding from my own numbers: yes. 56.3% (277 of 492) against 53.7% (1,058 of 1,971), both measured.
- Named person who acts: yes. A sports bettor (role).
- Decision with timing: partly. The timing ("before kickoff") and the decision are named, but the sentence reports what the bettor backs, not what they should do or what will happen. As written, it is closer to a description than a claim.

## Why a skeptic would disagree
A 56.3% win rate is not the same as a winning bet. Moneyline odds already price in which side is more likely to win, so if the odds on those home teams already reflect the 56.3%, backing them returns nothing extra. My numbers do not include odds yet.

## My ask
> I am asking the sports bettor to adopt betting the moneyline on the home team with more rest before kickoff.

**Test**
- WHO: yes. The sports bettor (role).
- VERB: yes. "Adopt" is a decision verb.
- WHAT: yes. It matches my action.
- WHEN: partly. "Before kickoff" says when each bet is placed, not by when the bettor adopts the approach.

## Hypotheses and unknowns
- **HYPOTHESIS:** the home-rest edge is still there after the moneyline odds are taken into account. Not tested; odds are not in my numbers yet.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** the 2.6-point gap (56.3% against 53.7%) is more than chance. Not tested.
- **Planned, not counted yet:** the rest edge within spread bands, the rest effect by season, and splits by roof, surface, and weather.
- **No pivot yet:** I do not have an Excel pivot, so there are no pivot counts for my candidate cut.
- **Unknown:** whether a real bettor would confirm this decision and question. I have not spoken to one; everything about the bettor is my guess, not confirmed by them.
